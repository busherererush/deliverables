01. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how BUSHERERE ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in Kuyitale (the "App"). Kuyitale is an offline, number-trail puzzle game intended for people aged 13 and above. It does not require registration, login, or an internet connection to play.

02. Information we collect

We do not collect, transmit, or store personal information on our servers through Kuyitale. The App does not ask for your name, email address, telephone number, location, contacts, photographs, or other identifying details.

We do not use the App to collect analytics, advertising identifiers, usage telemetry, or crash reports. We do not sell or share personal information with advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties.

03. Game progress stored on your device

To let you continue playing, Kuyitale may save gameplay information such as unlocked levels, completed puzzles, and scores in local app storage on your device. This information stays on your device and is not sent to BUSHERERE.

You can remove locally stored progress by clearing the App's storage in your device settings or uninstalling the App, subject to your device's backup and restore behavior. Because we do not receive this information, we cannot access or delete it remotely.

04. Device permissions and connectivity

Kuyitale is designed to operate without an internet connection and without requesting runtime access to sensitive device features such as your camera, microphone, contacts, or location. Normal operating-system functions required to display and run the App may still apply.

05. Advertising and third-party services

Kuyitale does not display third-party advertisements, use advertising or analytics SDKs, or require social media sign-in. The game does not intentionally transmit user data to external services. If you choose to email us, that communication takes place through your chosen email provider, outside the App's gameplay.

Installing or updating the App through an app store may involve information processed independently by that store under its own privacy terms; that processing is not performed by Kuyitale.

06. Security, retention, and deletion

Our privacy approach is to minimize data handling: the game does not maintain a remote player database. Local gameplay progress remains on the device until you clear the App's data or uninstall it, subject to device-level backup and restore settings.

When you contact us by email, we receive the information you voluntarily include in your message. We use it only to respond to your inquiry and manage the related correspondence. You may request deletion of your correspondence by emailing us; we will address the request subject to applicable legal obligations.

07. Age suitability

Kuyitale is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other players through the App. The age recommendation is not a substitute for the official content rating assigned through the relevant app store.

08. Changes to this policy

If Kuyitale's features or privacy practices change, we will update this page and revise the effective date. Any new collection, sharing, or use of personal data would be disclosed before the relevant changes are introduced, as required by applicable law and platform policy.
