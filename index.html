Busherere Innovation Hub is an initiative of TUEZUULE INITIATIVE, a community-focused organisation committed to people whose potential too often goes unseen.

After discovering promising skills among young innovators, including software development, we set out to support their ambition. Busherere is a place to recognise that talent and help it move forward.
